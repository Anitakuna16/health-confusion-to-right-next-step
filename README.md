# Health Confusion to Right Next Step

AI-powered health guidance assistant that helps users understand their health concerns and guides them from confusion to the right next step.

## Problem Statement

### Health: From Confusion to the Right Next Step

People often experience health concerns but may not know what their symptoms could mean or what they should do next. This project aims to provide simple, educational health guidance and help users identify an appropriate next step.

## Solution

HealthNextStep AI collects basic information about a user's health concern, existing conditions, medications, and duration of symptoms. It then provides easy-to-understand educational information and guidance on when professional medical care may be appropriate.

## Key Features

* Health concern and symptom input
* Existing conditions information
* Current medication information
* Simple educational health guidance
* Appropriate next-step suggestions
* Professional-care guidance
* Urgent-care warnings when appropriate

## Technology Used

* AWS PartyRock
* Generative AI
* Prompt Engineering

## Important Note

This application provides educational information only. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
